Append-Only Double-Entry Ledger

Every rupee is tracked down to the exact paise without floating-point inaccuracies. Atomic balance increments guarantee strict mathematical balance ($\Sigma\text{Debits} == \Sigma\text{Credits}$) and zero drift under concurrent stress.

Indian GST Statutory Engine

Dynamic determination of intra-state (CGST + SGST) vs inter-state (IGST) split disbursements across solar equipment (HSN 8541 @ 12%, 8504 @ 18%) and transport freight (HSN 9965 @ 18%) with Mod-36 checksum verification.

Dual-Layer Idempotency Barrier

Fast-path Redis atomic reservation backed by PostgreSQL hash constraints prevents duplicate debiting and prevents double-spend anomalies across network timeouts or concurrent client retries.
